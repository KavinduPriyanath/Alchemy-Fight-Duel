HOW TO PLAY

MASTER THE ELEMENTS

  1. Tap cards to fill the three alchemy slots. Each card spends mana. The first two elements appear in your hands; the third mixes into a spell ball.
  2. Once filled, you have five seconds to change your selection. Tap × on a slot to return a card and its mana.
  3. After the lock, hold and drag anywhere in the arena to look around. Center the reticle on the rival or a highlighted summon tile, then release to cast.
  4. Use End Turn to let your units march and the rival act. Reduce the rival to 0 health to win.

Elemental reactions: Fire + Water = Vaporize (1.5×); Water + Lightning = Electrocharge (1.5×); Fire + Poison = Combustion (1.6×); Earth + Wind = Shatter (1.35×); Water + Wind = Freeze (1.2× and skips a unit action); Fire + Earth = Molten (1.3×). Fire burns for 2 and Poison deals 3 damage on each of the next two turns. Your first card sets the attack element.
